Download Speculae for Windows

🛡️ Focus on Privacy

Many journal apps send your data to a cloud. Speculae acts differently. The software stores every word you write in a local database on your computer. This means no company views your private thoughts. Even when the AI agents work to provide balance or meditation feedback, they operate within the borders of your personal machine.
